# Content Plan — S1 Multi-Language Campaign

## Strategy Summary

Two-channel, two-language product launch. LinkedIn for the professional English and Spanish audience, Twitter for real-time engagement in both languages. All four assets are independent social-copy pieces that share the same product message but are tailored per channel and language.

## Channel Language Matrix

| Channel | Language | Tone | Asset Count |
|---------|----------|------|-------------|
| linkedin | en | professional, confident, fact-led | 1 |
| linkedin | es | professional, confident, fact-led | 1 |
| twitter | en | punchy, conversational, newsy | 1 |
| twitter | es | punchy, conversational, newsy | 1 |

## Asset Plan

| id | title | asset_type | channel | language | source_asset | depends | publish_at | external_gate | notes |
|----|-------|-----------|---------|----------|-------------|---------|------------|---------------|-------|
| A1 | LinkedIn launch post (en) | social-copy | linkedin | en | null | [] | null | null | Primary English announcement |
| A2 | LinkedIn launch post (es) | social-copy | linkedin | es | null | [] | null | null | Spanish translation of primary |
| A3 | Twitter launch thread (en) | social-copy | twitter | en | null | [] | null | null | English thread |
| A4 | Twitter launch thread (es) | social-copy | twitter | es | null | [] | null | null | Spanish thread |

## Timeline

All four assets are independent (no cross-asset dependencies). A1 and A3 (English) can be dispatched first. A2 and A4 (Spanish) follow. For initial launch, publish English assets 30 minutes before Spanish to give the primary audience first visibility.

## Assumptions

- Spanish translations will be generated by the social-copy worker with `language: es`; quality depends on the model's translation fidelity.
- No external gate or embargo constraints.
- Brand voice is consistent across languages; tone mapping is the same.
- The twitter `asset_type` is `social-copy` (twitter-specific worker not yet implemented; signal-worker routes it to the generic social-copy worker).
